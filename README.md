# Sunset Heist Cover Studio

[![gta6-cover-art-01](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01?source=github&medium=readme&campaign=gta6-cover-art-01) [![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/dickycan42/gta6-cover-art/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01/leaderboard) [![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/dickycan42/gta6-cover-art/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)

Turn your photos and a caption into a multi-panel poster-style cover, graded like a neon sunset, and export it as a PNG to share. It runs fully offline in a desktop browser: your photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded anywhere.

## What it does

- **Any photo** — load one or several photos from your machine, or try the two built-in samples. Click a panel to select it, drag to move its photo, scroll to zoom it, and assign any photo to any panel.
- **Five layouts** — four-panel grid, hero photo with three small panels, one photo full-bleed, a nine-panel grid, and tilted snapshots.
- **Five colour grades** — Sunset, Neon night, Golden hour, Violet dusk, and Teal coast — washed over every panel so the photos share one mood.
- **Poster lettering** — a bold display caption that shrinks itself to fit the title band, a glowing neon script subtitle, a halftone title block, a star meter, and a ribbon badge.
- **Three export sizes** — Square 1080 × 1080, Portrait 1080 × 1350, and Wide 1920 × 1080, each one click to a PNG.
- **Live preview and undo** — every change paints at once, and every step can be undone or redone.

## How to use it

1. Open the app (serve `app/` with any static server, for example `node serve.ts`, then visit http://localhost:8080).
2. Add your photos, or tap a sample photo to try it right away.
3. Pick a layout and a colour grade, write your caption and subtitle.
4. Click a panel, drag its photo into place, scroll to zoom.
5. Hit an export button to download the cover as a PNG.

## Originality

An original design. It uses no game artwork, no game logos, and no game fonts. The lettering is Anton and Pacifico, both shipped under the SIL Open Font License (their license texts sit beside the font files in `app/fonts/`). The sample photos were made with an image model for make.fun and are free to use.
